# Privacy Policy for Sweeping Clock

**Effective Date:** November 24, 2026
**Last Updated:** November 24, 2026
**Hosted URL (for Google Play):** https://alok0248.github.io/sweeping_clock/privacy/

**Sweeping Clock** ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy
explains how our Android application, **Sweeping Clock** (package
`com.aistudio.sweepingclock.kxmpzq`, including the Live Wallpaper, Home Screen
Widget and Screen Saver features), handles information when you use it.

---

## 1. Summary

- **We do not collect personal information.** The app has no accounts and no
  sign-in, and we never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts
  or precise location.
- **Your settings stay on your device.** Themes, hand styles, colours, presets and
  world-clock timezones are stored in a local database on your phone and are never
  sent to us.
- **The app shows a banner ad.** Ads are served by **Google AdMob**, which receives
  limited device and usage data in order to serve and measure them (section 3).
- **You control ad consent.** Where the law requires it, a consent form is shown
  before any ad loads, and you can change your choice at any time inside the app.

---

## 2. Information the app itself handles

| Data | Where it lives | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Clock settings, themes, hand styles, presets, chosen timezones | Local database on your device only | To remember your customisation |
| Your ad-consent choice | On your device, and with Google's consent service through the consent SDK | To respect your choice |

We operate no servers and hold no accounts, so there is no personal profile for us
to look up, share or lose.

---

## 3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app displays a **banner advertisement** supplied by **Google AdMob (Google
Mobile Ads SDK)**. To select, deliver, measure and protect ads, Google's SDK
collects and shares with Google:

- **Device and other identifiers** — including the Android advertising ID and app
  set ID
- **Approximate location** — estimated from your IP address (not from GPS)
- **App interactions** — such as app launch, taps and video views
- **Diagnostic and performance data** — such as app launch time, hang rate and
  energy usage

This information is used for **advertising, analytics and fraud prevention**, and
is encrypted in transit using TLS. We never receive your personal data from
Google; we only see aggregate, anonymous performance reports. Google's own
handling is described at
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and
https://policies.google.com/privacy

**Your choices:**

- **Consent:** in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and US states with applicable law, a
  consent message is presented before any ad is requested, and ads are not loaded
  until consent is resolved. You can reopen it in the app under
  **Settings → About & Privacy → Ad privacy options**.
- **Advertising ID:** Android Settings → Privacy → Ads lets you delete or reset
  your advertising ID or opt out of personalised advertising.
- **Where ads appear:** only while the app screen is open. The home-screen widget,
  the live wallpaper and the screen saver contain no ads.
- The app is free because of this single banner ad.

---

## 4. Permissions and why they exist

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `INTERNET`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` | Load the ad and check connectivity |
| `com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID` | The advertising identifier used by AdMob |
| `SET_WALLPAPER` | Set and render the live clock wallpaper |
| `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED` | Resume widget updates after the device restarts |
| `WAKE_LOCK` | Keep the display on while the app is used as a desk/nightstand clock |

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

Sweeping Clock is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect personal information from children. The app's ad requests are not
child-directed.

---

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

We store no personal data on any server, so there is nothing for us to delete on
your behalf. Local settings are removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall
the app. To stop ad-related data collection, delete or reset your advertising ID
in Android Settings, or uninstall the app.

---

## 7. Security

Data collected by the advertising SDK is encrypted in transit with TLS. Your local
settings are protected by Android's application sandbox.
