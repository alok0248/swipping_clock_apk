# Privacy Policy for Sweeping Clock

**Effective Date:** October 15, 2026
**Last Updated:** October 15, 2026
**Hosted URL (for Google Play):** https://alok0248.github.io/privacy/

**Sweeping Clock** ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Android application, **Sweeping Clock** (including the Live Wallpaper, Home Screen Widget, and Screen Saver features), handles information when you use it.

---

## 1. Summary

**Sweeping Clock does NOT collect, transmit, or share any personal data.** The app works completely offline: it does not even request the Android `INTERNET` permission, so it cannot send information over the network. The app has **no user accounts, servers, or subscriptions** — there is nothing to sign in to and no data held by us.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

- **No Personal Information:** We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or device identifiers.
- **No Usage Tracking:** The app contains no analytics, crash reporters, advertising SDKs, or trackers of any kind.
- **Local Customization Data Only:** Your customizations (selected clock themes, hand styles, colors, presets, and preferred world-clock timezones) are stored in a local database (`Room`) inside the app's private storage **on your device only**. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or to any third party.

---

## 3. Permissions

Sweeping Clock declares **no Android permissions** in its manifest — no storage, microphone, camera, location, phone, or internet permissions. Its features (home screen widget, live wallpaper, and screen saver) are standard Android components that the operating system binds to the app automatically; they require no permission prompts from you.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services

The app contains **no third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services** (no Google AdMob, Google Analytics, or Firebase Analytics data collection). The app makes no network requests of any kind. It does bundle standard open-source libraries for its user interface and local storage; none of these libraries collect, transmit, or share any data.

---

## 5. Data Storage, Backup, and Deletion

- All settings are stored locally in the app's private storage on your device.
- Android's automatic backup may include this local app data in **your own** Google account backup, governed by your device backup settings. We never receive or can access that backup.
- **To delete all data:** use "Clear storage" for the app in Android Settings, or simply uninstall the app. This permanently removes all local settings and presets.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

Sweeping Clock complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy. The app does not target children and does not collect personal information from children under 13 (or the local legal equivalent).

---

## 7. Security

Because all settings remain 100% local on your device, your information is protected by Android's native app sandboxing. No data is transmitted, so there is nothing to intercept.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
